Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEAL)

Logframes, indicators, data systems, and donor reporting

Build stronger programmes with monitoring, evaluation, and learning systems that turn project data into actionable insights. From results frameworks and indicator development to data collection, performance tracking, and donor reporting, I help organisations measure progress, demonstrate impact, and make evidence-informed decisions.

What I Offer

Results Frameworks and Logframes

Develop logical frameworks, theories of change, results chains, and performance measurement plans aligned with programme objectives and donor requirements.

Indicator Development

Define SMART indicators, establish baselines and targets, and develop indicator reference sheets to ensure consistent measurement of programme results.

Data Collection and Management

Design data collection tools, reporting templates, and monitoring systems that improve data quality, consistency, and accessibility.

Monitoring and Evaluation Plans

Establish monitoring schedules, evaluation approaches, data collection methods, and reporting processes to track implementation and outcomes.

Data Analysis and Visualisation

Translate programme data into meaningful insights through performance analysis, dashboards, and evidence-based recommendations.

Donor Reporting and Accountability

Prepare clear, evidence-based narrative and results reports that communicate progress, challenges, achievements, and lessons learned to funders and stakeholders.
