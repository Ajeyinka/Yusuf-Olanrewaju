## Project Management

### Research · M&E;

#### University of Bristol RCT on Fertility and Contraception

##### Hanovia Limited · Gates Foundation and World Bank

Managed the full programme lifecycle of a multi-state randomised controlled trial examining why women with fertility intentions fail to use contraception. Responsibilities spanned pre-field planning (inception report, budget development, survey instrument design, IRB compliance, MoH authorisations across 5 states), active field coordination (6,738 participants across 490 enumeration areas using SurveyCTO CAPI/ACASI, weekly reporting to Head of Department, Hanovia leadership, and University of Bristol PI), and post-field reporting (field data collection report and final client report).

##### • 5 Nigerian States • 6,738 Participants • 490 Enumeration Areas • 98% Validation Accuracy
