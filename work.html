About Me

Bridging Evidence, Funding, and Community

I am a pharmacist, public health researcher, and programme manager with over three years of experience designing, implementing, monitoring, and evaluating health and social development programmes across Nigeria and internationally.

I have coordinated a Gates Foundation and World Bank-funded randomised controlled trial across five Nigerian states, led grants strategy for a digital adolescent health platform in Oyo State, secured $100,000 for a women-focused menstrual health initiative, and developed over $3 million in funding proposals across federal agencies, foundations, and international funders. I am currently completing a Master of International and Sustainable Development at Miva Open University, Abuja, graduating August 2027.
